## Brand Visibility (new)

Brand Visibility measures how often AI assistants actually name a brand. AIScan generates 30 realistic niche prompts, runs each through a frontier AI model, then counts every brand mentioned. The report includes mention rate, share of voice ranking against all competitors, sample mentions, a plain-words summary, 5 concrete recommendations, and a ready-to-deploy fix pack (citable passages, FAQ schema, llms.txt sections for the 3 weakest areas). 15 USDC on-site, 1.95 USDC for agents via x402.
